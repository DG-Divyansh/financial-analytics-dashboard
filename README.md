# Financial Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive financial intelligence and KPI monitoring solution analyzing corporate revenue streams, operational expenses, gross/net margins, and cash flow dynamics across global business units to drive executive financial strategy.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Python**: Pandas, NumPy (Data pipeline, transformations, and cleaning)
- **SQL**: Relational database schema, revenue/expense aggregations, and trend queries
- **Power BI**: Interactive financial reporting, DAX KPI modeling, and executive scorecards

## 📈 Core Financial KPIs
- **Total Revenue & Expense Tracking**
- **Gross Profit Margin & Net Income Margin (%)**
- **Operating Cash Flow Generation**
- **Business Unit & Regional Profitability Benchmarks**
